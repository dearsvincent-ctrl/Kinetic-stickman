Extend the MOVE joystick fully or hold Shift + WASD
Punch / Kick
Tap, hold or slide onto PUNCH / KICK. F / G on keyboard. Strikes can miss; the dummy flashes only on active contact.
Block
Hold or slide onto BLOCK to guard and pause attacks or hold Q
Jump
Tap or slide onto JUMP. Press again in the air for the second jump or Space
Control layout
Settings → Edit Controls
Momentum recovery
Sharp back, diagonal and sideways turns plant the feet, compress the knees and push off. Steering stays immediate. Reversals do not attack; use Punch or Kick deliberately.
Directional double jump
On the second Jump, hold forward for a frontflip, backwards for a backflip, or centre MOVE to stay upright. Diagonals work. Direction is relative to your take-off travel/facing, not the screen. Both jumps use the same strength; landing restores both jumps.
Keep moving
Tap or hold Punch / Kick during recovery; move and steer throughout.
Reset view
Tap RESET VIEW or R

Use the joystick for air control. Punch and Kick work on the ground and in the air. Block takes priority.
